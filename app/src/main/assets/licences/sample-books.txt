Sample books

KoLib comes with three classics to try it with:

  Alice's Adventures in Wonderland, by Lewis Carroll (1865)
  Persuasion, by Jane Austen (1817)
  The Wisdom of Father Brown, by G. K. Chesterton (1914)

Their texts are in the public domain. Their covers were made with
KoLib's cover designer.

It also comes with The Lighthouse Cat, a short comic made for KoLib.

The covers and the comic are part of KoLib, under the same licence as
the app: the GNU General Public License 3.0.
